Three practical levels of implementation.

Start with one high-friction workflow, prove the value and then expand.

Level 1
Workflow QuickStart

One repetitive workflow that needs a fast and visible improvement.

  • One workflow and up to three applications
  • Forms, routing and notifications
  • One management dashboard
  • Two weeks of support

Level 2
AI Workflow Starter Kit

Two or three related workflows that should operate as one connected system.

  • Process mapping and redesign
  • Basic AI interpretation or document handling
  • Approvals, alerts and dashboard
  • User testing and one month of support
Build a Starter Kit
Level 3
Agentic AI Transformation Sprint

A business function redesigned around integrated workflows and controlled AI agents.

  • Business-process diagnostic
  • Three to five connected workflows
  • AI agent, assistant or document processing
  • Governance, knowledge transfer and optimisation
